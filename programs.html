PROGRAM QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about starting a training program.

Every member receives an initial biometric and movement screen. Beginners often begin with structured foundational blocks in either Fat Loss & Conditioning or 1-on-1 Personal Training to master form before advancing.
Performance and Elite VIP members receive complete access to all group barbell and conditioning programs. Dedicated 1-on-1 coaching is included in Elite VIP and available as an add-on.
Programs are periodized in 4 to 6-week training blocks with weekly load adjustments and biometric re-assessments every 30 days to avoid adaptation plateaus.
