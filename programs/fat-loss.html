7-DAY TRAINING SCHEDULE

LOWER BODY + METCON

Goblet Squats, Romanian Deadlifts, Sled Pushes & Battle Rope Intervals

⏱ 55 MIN HIGH (HR 155–170 BPM)
✓ Dumbbell Goblet Squats — 4 sets × 12 reps (45s rest)
✓ Dumbbell Romanian Deadlifts — 4 sets × 12 reps
✓ Turf Prowler Sled Pushes — 5 rounds × 30 meters (Active recovery)
✓ Battle Rope Alternating Waves — 4 rounds × 30 sec on / 30 sec off
HEAD COACH EXECUTION CUE:

"Keep your transitions between strength movements and turf intervals under 45 seconds to maintain high metabolic output."

ZONE 2 AEROBIC FLUSH

Low-Intensity Steady State Cardio, Nasal Breathing & Core Stabilization

⏱ 45 MIN MODERATE (HR 125–135 BPM)
✓ Concept2 Indoor Rower — 15 minutes @ steady pace
✓ Incline Treadmill Walk (10% incline, 4.5 km/h) — 20 minutes
✓ Plank Shoulder Taps & Deadbugs — 3 sets × 15 reps
✓ Hip Flexor & Hamstring Banded Stretches — 10 minutes
HEAD COACH EXECUTION CUE:

"Breathe strictly through your nose during this aerobic flush. Zone 2 builds mitochondrial density and burns pure fatty acids."

UPPER BODY + CONDITIONING

Dumbbell Push Press, Chest-Supported Rows, Incline Push-Ups & SkiErg Sprints

⏱ 55 MIN HIGH (HR 150–165 BPM)
✓ Dumbbell Push Press — 4 sets × 10 reps
✓ Chest-Supported Dumbbell Rows — 4 sets × 12 reps
✓ Deficit Push-Ups & Ring Rows — 3 sets × 12 reps
✓ Concept2 SkiErg Intervals — 6 rounds × 250 meters sprint
HEAD COACH EXECUTION CUE:

"Drive through your legs on the push press and lock out with full overhead stability before lowering under control."

MOBILITY & ACTIVE RECOVERY

Hip Openers, Thoracic Spine Flow, Soft Tissue Release & 8,000 Step Walk

⏱ 40 MIN LOW / RESTORATIVE
✓ Pigeon Stretch & World’s Greatest Stretch — 3 rounds
✓ Full-Body Foam Rolling on IT Bands & Lats — 15 minutes
✓ Gentle 8,000 Step Outdoor Walk
✓ Contrast Hot/Cold Shower Therapy
HEAD COACH EXECUTION CUE:

"Hydrate thoroughly with electrolytes today to replenish minerals lost during metabolic sweat sessions."

FULL BODY METABOLIC COMPLEX

Trap Bar Deadlifts, Kettlebell Swings, Box Jumps & Row Sprints

⏱ 60 MIN MAX (HR 165–180 BPM)
✓ Trap Bar Deadlifts — 4 sets × 8 reps
✓ Russian Kettlebell Swings (Heavy) — 4 sets × 15 reps
✓ Soft Plyo Box Jumps (Step down) — 3 sets × 10 reps
✓ Concept2 Row Sprints — 5 rounds × 300 meters max effort
HEAD COACH EXECUTION CUE:

"Snap your hips explosively on the kettlebell swing. Do not lift with your arms; let hip extension drive the bell upward."

TURF SLED & TEAM CIRCUIT

Prowler Relays, Farmer Walks, Medicine Ball Slams & Core Finisher

⏱ 50 MIN HIGH (HR 150–170 BPM)
✓ Prowler Sled Push & Pull Relay — 4 rounds × 40 meters
✓ Heavy Dumbbell Farmer Carries — 4 sets × 40 meters
✓ Overhead Medicine Ball Slams (10kg) — 4 sets × 15 reps
✓ Hanging Knee Tucks & Side Planks — 3 sets × 12 reps
HEAD COACH EXECUTION CUE:

"Maintain a rigid core and tall posture during the farmer carries. Grip hard and do not let your shoulders roll forward."

COMPLETE REST & RESTORATION

Full Nervous System Reset, Hydration Loading & Weekly Meal Prep

⏱ REST RECOVERY
✓ Complete Rest — Zero Structured Exercise
✓ Weekly Body Composition & Weight Review in Ironforge App
✓ Meal Prep & Macronutrient Planning for Upcoming Week
✓ 8+ Hours Uninterrupted Sleep Cycle
HEAD COACH EXECUTION CUE:

"Plan your meals for the upcoming week today. Nutrition adherence is 80% of long-term sustainable fat loss."
