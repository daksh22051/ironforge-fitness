7-DAY TRAINING SCHEDULE

Click each day to explore the exercise split, target reps, and coach cues.

PUSH (CHEST, SHOULDERS, TRICEPS)

Incline DB Press, Flat Barbell Bench, Lateral Raises, Overhead Extensions

⏱ 65 MIN HIGH (RPE 8–9)
✓ 30° Incline Dumbbell Bench Press — 4 sets × 8–10 reps (3-sec eccentric)
✓ Flat Barbell Bench Press (Tension Focus) — 3 sets × 8 reps
✓ Seated Dumbbell Lateral Raises — 4 sets × 12–15 reps + Drop set
✓ Overhead Rope Tricep Extensions & Dips — 3 sets × 12 reps
HEAD COACH EXECUTION CUE:

"Pause for 1 full second at the deep stretch position of the incline press to eliminate momentum and maximize fiber tearing."

PULL (BACK, REAR DELTS, BICEPS)

Lat Pulldowns, Chest-Supported T-Bar Rows, Face Pulls, Incline Dumbbell Curls

⏱ 65 MIN HIGH (RPE 8–9)
✓ Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown (Full Stretch) — 4 sets × 10 reps
✓ Chest-Supported T-Bar Row (Upper Back) — 4 sets × 8–10 reps
✓ Cable Face Pulls with External Rotation — 3 sets × 15 reps
✓ Incline Bench Dumbbell Bicep Curls (Supinated) — 3 sets × 10–12 reps
HEAD COACH EXECUTION CUE:

"Initiate every pulling movement with your scapulae before bending the elbows. Squeeze the lats hard at full contraction."

LEGS (QUADRICEPS & CALVES)

Barbell Squats, Hack Squats, Leg Extensions, Standing Calves Raise

⏱ 70 MIN MAX (RPE 9)
✓ High-Bar Barbell Squats (Quad Bias) — 4 sets × 8 reps
✓ Plate-Loaded 45° Hack Squat — 3 sets × 10 reps (Deep knee flexion)
✓ Seated Leg Extensions (Rest-Pause Protocol) — 3 sets × 12 reps
✓ Standing Single-Leg Calf Raises — 4 sets × 15 reps
HEAD COACH EXECUTION CUE:

"Elevate your heels slightly on the hack squat to drive maximum mechanical tension directly through the vastus medialis."

REST & NUTRITIONAL REFUEL

Targeted Protein Loading, Hydration, 30 min Gentle Mobility

⏱ REST RECOVERY
✓ Gentle Dynamic Full-Body Mobility Flow — 20 minutes
✓ Targeted Protein Benchmark (2.2g per kg bodyweight)
✓ Contrast Shower / Sauna Therapy Session — 15 minutes
✓ Hydration Protocol (3–4 Liters with Minerals)
HEAD COACH EXECUTION CUE:

"Muscle is built during recovery, not in the gym. Eat your prescribed calories and let your muscle glycogen stores replenish."

UPPER BODY HYPERTROPHY

Dumbbell Shoulder Press, Cable Flyes, Seated Cable Rows, Hammer Curls

⏱ 65 MIN HIGH (RPE 8–9)
✓ Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Overhead Press — 4 sets × 8–10 reps
✓ Low-to-High Cable Chest Flyes — 3 sets × 12–15 reps (Peak squeeze)
✓ Single-Arm Cable Lat Rows — 3 sets × 10 reps / side
✓ Standing Dumbbell Hammer Curls & Skullcrushers — 3 sets × 12 reps
HEAD COACH EXECUTION CUE:

"Focus on tempo control. Take 3 seconds to lower the weight on every single rep to stimulate maximal mechanical tension."

POSTERIOR CHAIN & HAMSTRINGS

Romanian Deadlifts, Lying Leg Curls, Hip Thrusts, Hanging Knee Raises

⏱ 60 MIN HIGH (RPE 8–9)
✓ Dumbbell Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs) — 4 sets × 10 reps (Deep hip hinge)
✓ Lying Leg Curls (Plate-Loaded) — 4 sets × 10–12 reps
✓ Barbell Glute Hip Thrusts — 3 sets × 10 reps (2-sec lock pause)
✓ Hanging Captain’s Chair Knee Raises — 3 sets × 15 reps
HEAD COACH EXECUTION CUE:

"Keep your shins vertical on the RDL and push your hips as far back as possible until your hamstrings are fully loaded."
