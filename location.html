VISITOR INFORMATION

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before visiting our facility.

Is parking available on-site?
Yes. We provide over 50 dedicated member parking bays directly outside the entrance with 24/7 security and complimentary valet assistance during peak morning and evening hours.
Do I need an appointment before visiting?
Walk-ins are always welcome during operational hours (6:00 AM – 10:00 PM). If you would like a guided coach tour or free biometric assessment, booking in advance via WhatsApp is recommended.
What landmarks should I look for?
We are located on Westgate Boulevard, directly adjacent to the YMCA International Club, approximately 5 minutes from Sindhu Bhavan Marg and ISCON Mega Mall.
Are day passes available for visitors?
Yes, single day passes (₹500) and 10-session flexible packages (₹3,500) are available at the front concierge desk with zero lock-in contracts.
